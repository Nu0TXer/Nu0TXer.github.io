## 👋 About Me

Hi! I'm a student at **Guangzhou University**, studying in the **Academician Fang Binxing Experimental Class** at the **Cyberspace Institute of Advanced Technology**.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence Security** and **Cybersecurity**, with a particular focus on the security, robustness, and trustworthiness of large language models (LLMs) and multimodal AI systems, as well as AI-driven cybersecurity.


**Links:** [GitHub](https://github.com/Nu0TXer) 

## 🔬 Research Interests

- 🛡️ **AI Safety & Security** — Jailbreak attacks, adversarial hallucination attacks, attack detection, and safety evaluation of multimodal large language models (MLLMs).
- 🌐 **Web Security** — Web application security, vulnerability discovery, and security assessment.
- 🔍 **AI-Driven Security Testing & Red Teaming** — Intelligent penetration testing, automated vulnerability assessment, and AI-powered red teaming.
- 🤖 **Agentic AI & Multi-Agent Systems** — LLM-based agents, multi-agent collaboration, autonomous workflows, and agent security.
